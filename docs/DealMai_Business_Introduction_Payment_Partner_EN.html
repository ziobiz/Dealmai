1. Business Overview (Service Description)

1.1 Overview

DealMai is an AI Sales Agent SaaS. Businesses connect an AI chatbot to their website or landing page to automate and support the sales flow— from customer inquiry and product guidance to booking/package recommendations and online payment.

Customers purchase packages (credit-based, one-time, or subscription). After payment is completed, an account and AI chatbot workspace are provisioned automatically. The platform supports both direct PG payments and partner sales channels (affiliate webhooks), with a multilingual UI in English, Thai, Japanese, and Korean.

  • Core value: connect “consult → recommend → pay → provision account” into one digital sales pipeline
  • Delivery model: cloud SaaS (web portal + admin console + AI chatbot workspace)
  • Payment flow: direct PG checkout and partner webhook sales for order settlement and account provisioning
  • Markets: Japan & Thailand in operation / Korea in preparation
Customer→AI chatbot consultation · product/booking guidance
Customer→Select package · online payment (PG / partner)
DealMai→Confirm order · auto-provision account/workspace · send guidance email
Customer→Portal login · use AI chatbot service · top-up / repurchase

1.2 Benefits for Merchants / Selling Businesses

  • 24/7 AI chatbot expands consultation and sales throughput versus staffing cost
  • Ideal for products that need explanation before payment (bookings, packages, credits)
  • Automatic account/workspace issuance after payment reduces drop-off and manual work
  • Multilingual support (JA/TH/KO/EN) helps expand customer touchpoints by country
  • Unified admin console for packages, orders, partners, payment events, and branding
  • Own customers and brand relationships—not an open-marketplace commission model
  • Broadens reach to seniors, persons with disabilities, and digitally excluded users—new demand segments

1.3 Benefits for End Customers — Inclusive, Voice-First Experience

As populations age and smartphones become ubiquitous, demand for online shopping, booking, and payment grows. Yet complex open-market and e-commerce UIs remain a high barrier for seniors, persons with disabilities, and digitally excluded users. DealMai is not click-heavy UI first; it is a conversational service guided by speech and dialogue, delivering a friendly, natural experience—similar to speaking with an agent on a traditional phone IVR/ARS line—available 24/7.

Core customer value
  • Speak & listen first: guide products, bookings, and payments by voice/conversation instead of tiny text, dense menus, and multi-step carts
  • More natural than classic ARS: not “press 1”; conversational Q&A like a live agent
  • 24/7 with no queue: continuous service nights and weekends (unlike staffed call-center wait times)
  • Patient with repetition: re-explains kindly—well suited to seniors and first-time users
  • Smartphone-ready: reduces app-install / signup friction; guides only the steps needed inside the dialogue
  • Digital inclusion: voice guidance expands access for users who struggle with visual or touch interfaces

Limitations disclosed candidly for payment-partner due diligence.

  • PG / KYC / refund / chargeback requirements must be met per country
  • Optimized for digital/service goods—not physical inventory logistics marketplaces
  • Korea is still in preparation; early traffic/revenue skews to Japan & Thailand
  • Partner-channel dependence may be affected by affiliate policy and settlement cycles
